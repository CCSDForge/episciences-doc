## Compatible Repositories & Open Access

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Supported Open Repositories" icon="open-book">
    Episciences operates on the **overlay journal** model, building upon existing preprint servers and open archives:
    - **HAL** (multidisciplinary open archive)
    - **arXiv** (physics, mathematics, computer science, etc.)
    - **Zenodo** (multidisciplinary research data and publication repository)
    - **bioRxiv / medRxiv** via import gateways
  </Card>

  <Card title="Diamond Open Access Model" icon="star">
    Episciences champions open science with no fees for authors (*no Article Processing Charges*) and no paywalls for readers. The platform is developed and maintained by the **CCSD** (CNRS, Inria, INRAE).
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
